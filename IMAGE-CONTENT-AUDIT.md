## 3. Mismatches found and corrected

### 3.1 CRITICAL — global `og:image:alt` was hardcoded and wrong (all pages)
- **Finding:** `og:image:alt` was hardcoded once in `index.html` to *"Golden
  Sahara desert dunes at sunset with camel caravan — Morocco Grand Adventure"*
  and was never overridden per route by the prerenderer or the runtime head
  manager. On **all 738 prerendered pages** the OG image's alt described a
  Sahara camel caravan regardless of the actual subject (riad breakfast,
  Hassan II Mosque, Fes tannery, Chefchaouen medina, etc.).
- **Correction:** Added `ogImageAlt()` to `route-metadata.ts` — returns the
  exact catalog alt for `/images/catalog/*` photos, otherwise a humanized
  filename — and wired it into `scripts/prerender.ts` (injectHead) and
  `src/components/seo/LocalizedHead.tsx` (runtime).
- **Result:** after re-prerendering, hardcoded mismatches dropped from **738 →
  1** (remaining 1 is the root post-Vite template, the no-JS fallback for `/`,
  which is a sahara panoramic).
- **Verification:** typed test via `tsx` + full site `tsc --noEmit` (clean) +
  regeneration of 1397 route files.

### 3.2 Food = destination mismatch (Casablanca)
- **Finding:** `FOOD_CATALOG` paired `casablanca` with
  `moroccan-mezze-couscous-tagine.webp`, but that image's `destinations` array
  was `['marrakech','fes','rabat']` (no casablanca) — contradicting the catalog.
- **Correction:** added `'casablanca'` to the mezze image's `destinations` in
  `imageCatalog.ts`, so the image, its section, and its destination align.
- All other food pairs already matched the catalog (`marrakech`, `fes`,
  `essaouira`, `rabat`, `merzouga` verified correct).

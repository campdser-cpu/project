## Notes
- No competitor (other than official ONMT platform) appears to run a **fully localized multilingual tour catalogue**; MGA's 11-locale hreflang architecture is differentiated.
- The most repeated competitor pattern MGA lacks is **Errachidia and Ouarzazate as Sahara departure hubs** (Moroccan Tour + Saha Tours) — reinforced by ONMT bundling `Errachidia–Midelt–Merzouga` and `Ouarzazate–Zagora–Tinghir`.
- Saha Tours and Moroccan Tour both sell **Zagora 2-day** and **"Grand South" / "Mil Kasbahs"** products MGA partially covers (`2-day-zagora-desert-marrakech`, `5-day-great-south-morocco`).

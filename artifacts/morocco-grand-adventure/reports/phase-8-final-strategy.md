## Q12. What should NOT be built `[INF]`
- Do **not** build per-destination × per-duration × per-language thousands of pages (thin content / cannibalization). The existing city×duration hub model already covers this without new templated pages.
- Do **not** add a second "Merzouga" or "Sahara" top-level hub — it would cannibalize `destinations/merzouga` and the guides; consolidate instead.
- Do **not** duplicate comparison intents (e.g. a second "Merzouga vs Zagora") — keep canonical on the existing comparison.
- Do **not** compete with Visit Morocco on pure official qualitative queries (best-time Full-Morocco, visa/currency) — reference, don't duplicate.

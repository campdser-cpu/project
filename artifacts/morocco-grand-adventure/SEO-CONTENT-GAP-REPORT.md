## Cannibalization watchlist [GSC-VALIDATE]
- `camel-trekking` hub vs. desert-tour pages mentioning camel trekking — hub informational-leaning, tours transactional.
- Blog `marrakech-to-merzouga-roadtrip` vs. `/tours/from-marrakech/*` — blog links into tours, does not compete on "book" intent.
- Merzouga destination page vs. Merzouga hub guides — destination = what/where; hub guides = how to do X.
